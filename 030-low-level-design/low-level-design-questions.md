# Low-Level Design (LLD) Interview Question Bank (SDE-2 Backend)

> **Target Audience:** Backend Engineers with 2–5 Years of Experience
>
> **Focus:** Amazon, Microsoft, Google, Uber, LinkedIn, Netflix, Atlassian, Adobe, Walmart Global Tech, PhonePe, Razorpay, Flipkart, Swiggy, Meesho, etc.
>

>
